Week 7 – Dashboard and Visualization
Components Built

Hourly consumption chart
Daily consumption chart
Weekly consumption chart
Monthly consumption chart
Device-wise usage chart (donut chart)

Tools Used

Matplotlib (chart generation)
Flask (serving charts as static images)
HTML, CSS, JavaScript (frontend display)

Charts Overview

Hourly: Average kWh per hour across 24 hours
Daily: Total kWh per day for last 30 days
Weekly: Total kWh per week for last 12 weeks
Monthly: Total kWh per calendar month
Device-wise: Estimated energy share per appliance

Smart Suggestions Feature
Energy-saving tips generated based on appliance selection and usage patterns.
Tips include AC temperature advice, geyser usage, peak hour avoidance, and LED switching.
