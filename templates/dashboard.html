Energy Saving Tips

Simple habits that can cut your electricity bill every month.

❄️

Set AC to 24°C

Every degree you lower the AC temperature adds about 6% to your energy bill. 24°C is the sweet spot for comfort and savings.

Save up to 24%
🚿

Turn Off Geyser After Use

Geysers keep reheating water even after you're done bathing. Switching it off saves ₹200–400 every month.

Save ₹200-400/month
🕖

Avoid Peak Hours (6 PM – 10 PM)

Electricity demand is highest in the evening. Running the washing machine or dishwasher late night or early morning saves on tariffs.

Save 10-15%
💡

Switch to LED Bulbs

LED bulbs use 75% less electricity than regular bulbs and last 10× longer. Replace all bulbs in one go for maximum savings.

Save 75% on lighting
🔌

Unplug Idle Devices

TVs, chargers, and set-top boxes on standby quietly consume power. Plugging them into a switchable power strip makes this easy.

Saves ~10% monthly
🫧

Full Loads Only

Run your washing machine and dishwasher only when fully loaded. A half-empty machine uses the same energy as a full one.

Save 20% per cycle
☀️

Use Natural Light

Keep curtains open during the day instead of turning on lights. A bright room costs ₹0.

Free savings
🌡️

Service Your AC Regularly

A dirty AC filter makes the unit work harder, using up to 15% more electricity. Clean it every month during summer.

Save 10-15%
